# Photo placeholders

The first six slots are the photographs supplied with the project (`memory-01.jpg` through `memory-06.jpg`). Replace them with your preferred photos while keeping the filenames. Slots 7–10 are illustrations; replace `memory-07.svg` through `memory-10.svg` with photos, then update `photoPaths` in `src/content.js`. JPG, PNG, and WebP work well. You can also upload up to ten photos from the camera button; browser uploads are resized and saved locally in this browser.

These simple illustrations are placeholders so the scrapbook has artwork before you add personal photos.
